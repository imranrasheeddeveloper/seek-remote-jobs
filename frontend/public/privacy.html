Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 May 2026

SeekRemoteJobs ("we," "our," or "us") operates seekremotejobs.com (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have regarding your personal data. By using the Site, you agree to the terms of this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require you to create an account or provide any personal information to use SeekRemoteJobs. However, certain data is collected automatically when you visit:

  • Log data: IP address, browser type, referring URL, pages visited, and time stamps. This data is collected by our hosting provider and is used only for security and performance monitoring.
  • Cookies and local storage: We store your cookie consent preferences in your browser's local storage so we do not ask repeatedly. No unique identifiers are transmitted to our servers.
  • Advertising data: If you consent, Google AdSense may place cookies to serve personalised ads. If you do not consent, only non-personalised ads are served.

2. Google AdSense and Advertising

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on the Site. Google uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our Site and other sites. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings or by adjusting your preferences using the cookie consent banner at the bottom of every page.

For more information on how Google collects and uses data, please review the Google Privacy Policy.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate and improve the Site and its search and filtering features
  • To monitor and ensure the security and stability of the Site
  • To serve relevant or non-personalised advertisements via Google AdSense
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party.

4. Third-Party Links

Every job listing on SeekRemoteJobs links to an external company careers page. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third-party websites. We encourage you to read the privacy policy of any website you visit through our links.

5. Cookies and Your Consent Choices

When you first visit the Site, a consent banner gives you the option to accept, deny, or customise cookie usage. Your choices are stored locally in your browser and applied on each subsequent visit. You can change your preferences at any time using the "Privacy & Cookie Settings" link in the footer.

The specific cookie categories are:

  • Ad storage: Allows Google to store ad-related cookies on your device.
  • Ad user data: Allows sending data about your visit to Google for ad measurement.
  • Ad personalisation: Allows Google to use your data to show you personalised ads.
  • Analytics storage: Allows analytics tools to store cookies for measuring usage patterns.

6. Data Retention

We do not store personal data on our servers beyond standard server log files, which are retained for up to 30 days for security purposes. Your consent preferences are stored only in your own browser and are not transmitted to us.

7. Children's Privacy

SeekRemoteJobs is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will promptly remove it.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights under applicable data protection law (including the GDPR for EEA residents and the CCPA for California residents) to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal data. Because we do not collect or store personally identifiable information beyond server logs, most requests can be fulfilled by clearing your browser cookies and local storage. For any other request, contact us at privacy@seekremotejobs.com.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by a new "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the Site after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.

10. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: privacy@seekremotejobs.com
